# Startup deals

**The job:** the programs this founder gets into, the order to apply in, the ones everyone lists that won't work for them, and what the kit costs a month. The list is [references/deals.md](references/deals.md), each row dated.

## 1. Ask the five, once

Read their message. Ask whatever is still missing in one message, then wait for the answers, because nearly every program turns on them. Once you know their stage, team size and country, answer; a missing investor name goes at the end, as what could open a partner route:
1. How much have you raised, and from whom (none, angels, a VC)?
2. Are you in an accelerator or backed by a VC? Which one?
3. When was the company founded?
4. How many people are on the team?
5. Which country is the company registered in?

## 2. Match

A program fits when they meet its "Who gets in". Sort what you'll show:
- **Apply first:** the ones that fit, most value for the least effort first, each with the rule that let them in.
- **Not for you:** the closed, ended or out-of-reach ones they'd likely try, with the reason in a few words.

Rows tagged secondhand or unverified are leads: say so beside them.

## 3. Re-read each one you show

Open the program's own page (the Page column; search for it if the link moved) and use what it says today, with today's date. When a page won't load, keep the list's row and its date. Today's date goes only beside a page you fetched in this run; for the rest, say once at the top: "From our list dated [the dates], not re-checked today." Here a number comes from the program's own page, with the date it was read.

## 4. Price the kit

Sum "The kit's monthly price" with their real choices: their Vamos plan (Pro if they haven't said, and say it's assumed), Zernio's 2 free accounts, and ElevenLabs at $0 with the grant or Starter's price without it. Only official prices go in the total; a secondhand one sits beside it.

## The answer

<example>
Two founders in Lisbon, bootstrapped, a year old, paying users:

From our list dated 2026-11-10 and 2026-11-11, not re-checked today.

**Apply first**
1. **ElevenLabs Startup Grant**: 12 months of voice generation free. You're under 25 people. elevenlabs.io/grants-application (read 2026-11-10)
2. **fal.ai Startup Program**: $1,000 in credits. Open to teams in Europe with traction, and you have paying users. fal.ai/startup-program (2026-11-10)
3. **AWS Activate Founders**: $1,000, up to $5,000. You're self-funded and under 10 years old. aws.amazon.com/startups/credits (2026-11-10)
4. **TikTok Ads for new advertisers**: spend-to-get credit in your ad account's banner, new accounts only. (2026-11-10)

**Not for you**
- Claude for Startups: closed, the cohort is full (2026-11-11). Anthropic's API credits now come only through a partner VC.
- Brex and Mercury perks: for US-registered companies.
- Google Cloud's AI tier: for VC-funded startups.

**Your kit: $69 a month.** Claude Pro $20, Vamos Pro $49, Zernio $0 for 2 accounts, Apify free plan $0, ElevenLabs $0 with the grant (each read on its own page, 2026-11-09 to 11-11). A video generator such as Kling Standard, about $6.99, is a secondhand price, so it's not in the total.

</example>

Keep to the example's size: a line or two per program, the best four to six, and the three or four traps they'd most likely try; the rest stay in the list. Give the links and let them apply; a form or an email goes out only from them.

# hooks: Cracked marketer's words

Each edit below is Content System's words in `plugins/vamos/skills/hooks`, then the words Cracked marketer's copy has instead. `python3 scripts/build-skills.py` applies them and fails when the old words are gone.

A brand writes hooks for its ads as well as its posts, so the proven lines include the opening lines of the Meta ads its category keeps paying to run, and a hook a creator will film goes to a brief.

~~~old SKILL.md
built from real hooks that beat their creators' usual in that format, niche and kind of product. Also
~~~

~~~new
built from real hooks that beat their creators' usual in that format, niche and kind of product, and from the opening lines of the Meta ads your category keeps running. Also
~~~

~~~old SKILL.md
views, the multiple, the link and the date.
~~~

~~~new
views, the multiple, the link and the date. [unless Vamos settings has Ads off: Then the 20 Meta ads still running in [category] in [market] that have run longest, 30 days or more, from [competitors] and the category's other advertisers. Give each ad: the advertiser, the Ad Library link, days running, copies, the format, the first line said and the words on screen in the first frame (a static: the words on the image and its headline), and whether you read them from the ad itself or only from its copy.]
~~~

~~~old SKILL.md

~~~new
The words are in the answer's text; the views and links are in its rows. An ad's line read from the ad itself is a proven hook, ranked by days running where a post ranks by views. Vamos opens only some ads, so a line read only from an ad's copy is labelled "ad copy": the text above the ad, not its opening.
~~~

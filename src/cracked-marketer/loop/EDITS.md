# loop: Cracked marketer's words

Each edit below is Content System's words in `plugins/vamos/skills/loop`, then the words Cracked marketer's copy has instead. `python3 scripts/build-skills.py` applies them and fails when the old words are gone, so a shared edit can't drop one silently.

The description names the creators' week only where there is a creator program to run, and the research pack only where winning-ad-ideas is there to make it.

~~~old SKILL.md
a weekly content log, and the videos
~~~

~~~new
a weekly content log with your paid creators' results, a Monday research pack of judged ad concepts, and the videos
~~~

~~~old SKILL.md
to automate your content, keep research running
~~~

~~~new
to automate your content, run a creator program's week by itself, get a research pack every Monday, keep research running
~~~

The research pack is a brand strategist's Monday: the ad account's week, what customers say and ad concepts judged blind, built from content-log, playbook and winning-ad-ideas.

~~~old SKILL.md
all drafted for them to send.
~~~

~~~new
all drafted for them to send.

Offer the research pack on the content log's day, an hour after it: the content log's ad week, what their customers say (read again each month) and winning-ad-ideas' concepts, kept by its three blind judges, waiting at To review with their codes. The ad week needs that week's two Ads Manager exports in the project folder before the content log runs, and only a Claude Code desktop task sees that folder; elsewhere the pack runs without it and says so. Taken, the Loop line adds `research pack [that day] [an hour after the content log]`, and its task says `run pack`.
~~~

~~~new
2026-11-12), the research pack up to 60 (up to 110 when its monthly customer read is due); saves are free.

The digest opens on the account's week from that Reviews row: spend and CPA against last week, or "no export this week".

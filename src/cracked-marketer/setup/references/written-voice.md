# A written voice

When nobody talks on camera for the brand, its voice is how it writes: the site, the captions and the ad copy. A founder who talks on their own account has a talking voice, so step 3 reads that account the usual way.

## The ask

In step 3, parts 1 and 4 become these; drop part 1's Meta ads when ads are off:
> 1. Read [website], the captions of my last 40 posts on [accounts] from the last 6 months, and my Meta ads' headlines and body text, and quote 10 of the captions and 5 of the ads in full. [when `voice` is false or they asked for a new one: Rebuild my voice guide from how we write, quoting our real opening lines, calls to action and phrases.]
> 4. List the facts we state (numbers, results, prices, guarantees, who it's for), each word for word with the link it's from.

## Your own work

- **The numbers:** `python3 scripts/textstats.py profile` on the quoted captions and ads as `{"text"}` items (a row holds only the first 150 characters, `caption_head` or `body_head`).
- **Voice examples:** five of the quoted captions or ads, spread over formats and stages, one top performer among them.
- **Vamos settings:** the voice's state, "written from the site and N captions and ads".
- **The test:** write a caption for a held-out post's idea beside its real caption, then ask "Which lines aren't you?"
- **Too little:** under 10 captions and ads in all, mark it thin, as voice.md says.

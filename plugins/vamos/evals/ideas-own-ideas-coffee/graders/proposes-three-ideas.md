The user asked which of their own three ideas to make.
PASS if the reply gives a verdict on each of the three and proposes at least three ideas the user could film in all: the user's ideas it says to make, and the ways it gives to make them theirs, count.
FAIL if it leaves one of the three without a verdict, or proposes fewer than three ideas, or ends without verdicts (for example, because a tool call failed).

RIP's voice guide: a founder who has lived the trap, blunt and energetic; short punches ("Hey. Listen.") between longer reasoning lines; contractions ("wanna", "gonna"); numbers before claims; a no-pressure close ("You don't have to pay me to do this… But the point here is…") or "comment [keyword], and I'll send you access".
PASS if the rewrite reads like that founder talking, keeps about the draft's length (90 to 130 words), and has no em dashes or "it's not X, it's Y" lines.
FAIL if it reads like polished marketing copy, gains new claims or numbers, or is much longer or shorter.

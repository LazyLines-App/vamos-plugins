The user asked whether two of their own ideas are worth making.
PASS if the reply gives a verdict on each of the two and proposes at least three ideas the user could film in all: the user's ideas it says to make, and the ways it gives to make them theirs, count.
FAIL if it leaves one of the two without a verdict, or proposes fewer than three ideas, or ends without verdicts (for example, because a tool call failed).

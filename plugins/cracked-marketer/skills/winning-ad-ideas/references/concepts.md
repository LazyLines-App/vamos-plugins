# Concepts, worked

Five sources from Meta's Ad Library, each remade for a made-up brand: Strum, a guitar-lessons app (US, free trial, "learners play their first song in 9 days on average", which the user gave). The days are as read on the dates shown; yours come from this run's rows.

## A static, from a static

**From:** vidIQ, [ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1010481305111214), 114 days (read 2 Nov), static.
Its device: two real cards from the product, side by side, the number jumping between them: "312 → 2.4M Views".

**The picture:** two real screens from Strum's progress page, the same learner on day 1 and day 9: "0 songs" on the left, "1 song" with the song's name on the right.
**The line:** "Day 1 → Day 9. / Your first song." Headline field: "Start free".

Why this shape: the number jump is the source's whole device, so the picture keeps it and the user's own number goes in. "9 days" is the user's figure; without it the line would read "Day 1 → Day [N]".

## A video, from a video

**From:** ELSA Speak, [ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1844474886210761), 225 days (read 5 Nov), video, 56 s.
Its device: a podcast set, an interviewer who doubts the guest ("You moved here three months ago and didn't speak English?"), the app answering the doubt at 0:11, a score, the free trial.

**The picture:** two people at podcast mics, a guitar on the guest's knee.
**The line:** said and on screen, "Six weeks in and you're playing that?"
**Beats:** the guest plays eight bars; at 0:10 the phone shows Strum's lesson path and the song's score; the interviewer asks what it costs, the guest says "[your price]" and "start free".

A real learner plays the guest, or a presenter says it's a demo. Nobody acts as a customer.

## A static whose picture Vamos couldn't read

**From:** a language-learning app, [ad](link from the rows), 310 days, static, picture unread. Its words: headline "Still saying you'll learn it someday?", body "Someday isn't on the calendar. Ten minutes a day is."

**The picture:** built from its words, since its image is unread, and the reply says so: the question in big type, Strum's lesson path below it.
**The line:** "Been meaning to play for years?" Image words under it, the source's length: "Years go by. Your first song takes nine days." Headline field: "Start free".

Why it stays: 310 days is the longest runner in the set, and its words carry the device even without the image. The shape is kept (a question that names the delay, then a short flip) and no word is shared.

## A video whose line names a result the user hasn't given

**From:** a fitness app, [ad](link from the rows), 260 days, video, 18 s. Its device: an ice lolly melting on a gym bench, said and on screen "Craving a beach-ready body? ☀️".

**The picture:** a guitar case open on a park bench, a phone on the strings.
**The line:** said and on screen, "Dreaming of `[what your learners play by week 6]`? 🎸"
**Beats:** the phone shows Strum's lesson path; a learner plays the opening bars; "Start free" and the site.

Why the slot: "beach-ready body" is the source's result. Strum's user gave "a first song in 9 days" and nothing about week 6, so the result goes in as a slot, never one written here.

## Set aside, with the reason

- A 500-day catalog carousel of six product cards: a feed's days measure stocking, not a concept.
- A 14-day ad from the same competitor: new. Used only when fewer than 10 sources have run 30 days, labelled "14 days".
- A shorter-running ad with the same two people on the same podcast set and a different line: the same picture to Meta, so it counts once, as the 225-day ad.

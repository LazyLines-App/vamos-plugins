# The creator sheet

One row per creator, Main list first, best fit first, then Near miss. In Google Sheets, Main and Near miss are two tabs; in a CSV, the `list` column holds which.

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| list | Main, or Near miss: [what it misses] |
| handle | @handle, linked to the profile |
| platform | TikTok, Instagram or YouTube |
| tier | small (under 10K followers), mid (10K to 100K), big (over 100K) |
| followers | as Vamos gave it |
| usual views | Vamos's figure, as given ("not computed" when Vamos said so) |
| country | as Vamos gave it |
| on topic | "4 of 6", or "not checked" |
| talks to camera | "5 of 6", or "not checked" |
| format | Vamos's words; "inferred from captions" when Vamos said it watched none |
| deals seen | brands, codes or partnership ads Vamos named; a category brand is flagged |
| contact | the bio email exactly as Vamos gave it; else the link in bio; else DM |
| contact source | bio, link in bio, contact button (Apify), or none |
| outreach | Email, Instagram DM, or DM first (EU and UK creators, since cold email to a personal address is weak ground under GDPR) |
| found as | posting about the brand, posted for a rival, works with 2+ brands, breakout, or lookalike |
| why they fit | Vamos's reason, short |
| first message | below |
| read | the date of the Vamos answer |

## The first message

Two or three sentences the user sends from their own account. It names something real from this creator's posts in Vamos's answer (a series, a video and its date), says what the brand is and the pay per video (a `[$ per video]` slot when the user hasn't said), and asks one question. No link: it goes in after they reply. Email when there's one; else an Instagram DM, after checking the Instagram profile is the same person, since a matching handle is often someone else; a TikTok DM last, a few a day.

> Hi Ana, your Sunday starter-feeding videos are exactly how people use our kit. We're Crumb, a sourdough starter kit for first-time bakers, and we'd pay [$ per video] for short videos of you baking with it. Open to a paid test?

> Hey Theo, your "loaf 1 vs loaf 30" series is the job our kit does. We're Crumb (a starter kit for beginners) and pay [$ per video] for creator videos. Want the details?

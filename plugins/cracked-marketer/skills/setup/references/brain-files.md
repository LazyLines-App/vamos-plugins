# The Brain files setup writes

Each is a Brain file in Vamos, so every host, every Vamos chat and every scheduled run reads the same one, and the user can open and edit it at `https://app.getvamos.ai/brain`. Keep each short: Vamos's agent reads them on every chat.

## Tracked accounts

One line per account, grouped:
- **Yours:** each platform's handle, the site, the Meta page when ads are on.
- **Competitors** (about 4), **next door** (about 4: same audience, another product), **bigger** (about 2): each with handles, site, Meta page when ads are on, and why it's tracked in a few words ("sells the same thing to gyms", "sells something else to the same buyers").
- **Creators you work with**, when they named any.

## Things you've said

Each first-person fact from their transcripts (numbers, history, people, results, opinions) word for word, with its video link and timestamp. A fact you couldn't find in a transcript you hold is marked "check with them". Add "Proof you can use": results, data, access or customers they can show.

## What works for me

Under about 400 words. At setup, from their own rows:
- their usual and the date it was read;
- 2 to 4 traits of their top quarter against their bottom half, each counted ("number-first openers: 5 of 9 top posts, 1 of 18 bottom ones"), each marked Hunch (under 3 posts) or Confirmed (3 or more, from different weeks);
- their best and worst post, linked, with multiples.

## Marketing plan

How a stranger becomes a buyer, so every idea knows its job.
- **The offer:** what they sell, prices, the offer people buy first, where they buy.
- **The goal** for the next 90 days, with any launch or season coming, dated.
- **The asks:** each way a post asks people to act (link, comment keyword, code, free guide, trial) and what's at the end of it.
- **The stages,** each with its share of their posts and its median multiple: **reach** (top of the funnel: for strangers, no ask beyond follow), **trust** (middle: proof, how-to, behind the scenes, customers, collabs) and **buy** (bottom: the product shown working, objections answered, the offer and its ask).
- **The thin stage** for their goal, in one line ("Sales goal, and 2 of 34 posts ask anyone to buy").

## Vamos settings

- Setup date, and the voice's state (built from N talking videos, or thin).
- Voice numbers: the JSON `textstats.py profile` printed, on one line.
- The board's link and kind (Notion, Sheet, local or this file).
- Approval: Ask me after ideas (the default until `/cracked-marketer:loop` asks).
- Ads: on or off.
- Filming: face yes or no, voice yes or no, alone or with others, editing they can do.
- Off limits: topics, claims and words they won't use; their positions on their field.
- Not for me: formats and ideas they turned down or have done, each dated.
- Your calls: each Not for me note as a dated line with its reason.
- Pages: each page's link with its date, newest first.

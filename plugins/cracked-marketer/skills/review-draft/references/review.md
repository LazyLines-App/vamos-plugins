# The review

Two readers: the user, who decides, and the creator, who gets the notes. The notes are the part they paste, so they're written to the creator; the rest is for the user.

| Part | Holds |
|---|---|
| Title line | `## Draft review · [creator] · [the video] · round [n] of [agreed]` |
| **Verdict** | Pass or Fix, and why in one line. "Pass once you've confirmed …" when a must-have is to confirm |
| **The approve-when list** | Each must-have from the brief (the hook word for word, the hook on screen from the first frame, the product on screen by 3 s, the ★ line as written, the disclosure, and anything else its approve line or "Fixed:" names): ✓, ✗ or to confirm, with the evidence |
| **Notes to send** | One per ✗, blockers first, each `0:04 · …` (minutes:seconds) and one or two sentences: what to change and to what, the exact words for a line. Open on one thing that works. Then other parts of the brief they missed, marked "suggestion" |
| **Your notes** | Each of the user's own notes, tagged "in the brief (part n)" or "new ask", with one line on what to do with it |
| **What this couldn't check** | What this host couldn't see, and what would settle it |

The disclosure has two halves. #ad in the caption (or said or on screen) is checked now. The platform's paid-partnership label is switched on when posting, so a file never shows it: it is "check on the live post", which doesn't hold up a pass.

**Verdict: Fix.** Three must-haves are out: Potly isn't on screen by 3 s, the ★ line changed, and the caption has no #ad. The hook and the screens are right.

- ✗ Potly on screen by 3 s: the 3.0 s frame is still the kitchen; the app first shows in the 5.0 s frame.

- ✓ The app screen readable: Bills, Fun money and the third pot read at full size from 5.0 to 17.8 s.
- ✗ Disclosure: no #ad in the caption she sent. The paid-partnership label: check on the live post.
- ✓ 9:16 at 1080 × 1920.

**Notes to send Dana**
> Love the hook, it lands word for word and the on-screen text matches. Three changes and it's done:
> 1. **0:00–0:04** · Get Potly on screen by 3 seconds: open the app while you're still saying the hook, so the notification and the app are in the first shot.
> 2. **0:11** · Say the Potly line exactly as written: "Potly moves it the second I'm paid, so the rest is mine to spend." It's the one line we need word for word.
> 3. **Caption** · Add #ad, and switch on the paid-partnership label when you post.

**Your notes**

**What this couldn't check:** the paid-partnership label, until it's posted.

# Running a winner as an ad

When a creator's post wins, the cheapest way to scale it is an ad from their own handle: a TikTok Spark ad or an Instagram (or Facebook) partnership ad. That has to be agreed before anyone films. Later, the ask for the code has to be one the creator trusts. The user asks, the creator sends the code, and the user pastes it into Ads Manager. Nothing here touches an ad account.

## The terms, before anyone films (our default)

- **What:** you may run any video they post for you as an ad from their handle, through a code they make for that one post. You get no login, no access to their account and no posts in their name.
- **Window:** 60 days per video from the day they make its code, renewable at the same fee. TikTok's code comes in 7, 30, 60 or 365 days, so 60 days lets the code and the deal end together. Instagram's code has no end date, so you stop the ad on day 60.
- **Fee:** 30% of that video's base, paid when you ask for its code, so only the videos you run cost it. This is our proposal, inside SideShift's and Khasabo's published ranges below (Sequential's row gives a window, not a fee).
- **Edits:** the ad runs the post as it was posted. A cut-down or a new caption needs their raw files and their OK.
- **Not included:** account-level access (ads from their handle with videos they didn't post) and exclusivity. Each is asked for and priced on its own.
- **Budget:** keep the fee for 3 to 5 videos a month in reserve, for the ones that win.
- **Terms they already agreed stand.** When the program has an ad-use window and fee, use those and offer this default only to creators who haven't agreed one. A window that isn't one of TikTok's lengths (90 days, say) takes the next length up, and you stop the ad on the agreed day.

## How each code works

| | TikTok Spark ad | Instagram partnership ad |
|---|---|---|
| The creator makes it | On the video: ⋯, Ad settings, agree to the terms, turn on Ad authorization, Generate, pick 7, 30, 60 or 365 days, Copy code | On the reel or post: ⋯, Partnership label & ads, turn on Get partnership ad code, Copy |
| It covers | that one video, for the days they picked | that one post, for up to 2 brands at a time |
| It ends | by itself when the days run out (they can extend it in the same menu) | never by itself: you stop the ad. Turning the toggle off stops only new ads, and one already running keeps going |
| You paste it | TikTok Ads Manager: Tools, Creative library, Spark ads posts, Apply for authorization | Meta Ads Manager: on the ad, turn on Partnership ad and enter the ad code |
| Source | TikTok Ads help, last updated Oct 2025: https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-create-spark-ads-for-smart-campaigns-on-tiktok-ads-manager | Archive's creator guide, 2026-08-18: https://help.archive.com/en/articles/11028845-creator-s-guide-to-providing-ads-codes-for-instagram-paid-partnership-ad; Meta's creator blog, 2025-02-20: https://creators.instagram.com/blog/introducing-partnership-ads |

Menus move between app versions. On TikTok, ad authorization can also need turning on under Settings and privacy first. On Instagram, the account has to be a professional one, which is free to switch to. Code management is the known pain ("Spark Code management is the bottleneck", netinfluencer, above), so every code asked is recorded with its window.

## The code ask

The user sends it from their own account. It names the video, the kind of ad, the window and fee as agreed, the steps, and why it's safe. Fill the creator's name, their video (in their words) and the fee from the program; with no creator named yet, keep the brackets for the user to fill.

> TikTok: Hi [name], your [video] is our best yet, so we'd like to run it as a Spark ad from your account for 60 days, for the [fee] we agreed. On that video: tap ⋯, then Ad settings, agree to the terms, turn on Ad authorization, choose 60 days, tap Generate and send me the code. It covers that one video only. We get no login and can't post, edit or message from your account, the ad shows your handle and TikTok marks it Sponsored, and it ends by itself after 60 days.

> Instagram: Hi [name], your [reel] is our best yet, so we'd like to run it as a partnership ad for 60 days, for the [fee] we agreed. On that reel: tap ⋯, then Partnership label & ads, turn on Get partnership ad code, tap Copy and send it to me. It covers that one reel only. We get no login and can't post, edit or message from your account, the ad shows both our names, and we'll stop it on day 60. You can see it any time under Settings, Business tools and controls, Partnership ads.

With another agreed window, change the days; when TikTok has no such length, the ask says "choose 365 days" and "we'll stop it on day 90". When ad use wasn't agreed, the ask offers it ("for [$ for 60 days]"), and the program's terms get it before the next video.

**Record** each code asked in "Creator program: [brand]": the creator, the video, Spark or partnership, the window, the fee, and the day it was asked. The loop reminds the user 5 days before the window ends: renew it or stop the ad.

# The program

Notes from people who run creator programs and from our read of 134 short app demos that worked. Use them where they fit this brand and say whose each number is.

## How it starts

- **Nothing has won yet: a 30-day test, not a program.** 3 angles (from approved ideas) × 2 formats = 6 cells. Each cell goes up on 2 creators' accounts in weeks 1-2, so 12 videos from 3 or 4 creators. Week 3 reads them; week 4, every creator remakes what won.
- **Budget under about 10 videos** at the rate you'd pay: the team films the 6 cells from scripts on the brand's own accounts, and creators come in on the winners.
- **A post has already won:** skip the test. 3 or 4 creators each make their own version of it, with one thing changed.
- **Who:** 3 or 4 creators who belong to the niche, each with a post of their own that went well past their usual. Name the brands each one already posts for (the rows' paid-partnership flag); one who posts for a rival may be tied to it. Add more once a format works; the return per creator falls past about 30 (@maubaron, app program, Nov 2026).
- **Sold online:** hire more low-cost creators than you need and drop the ones who haven't delivered by week 2. Hit rates are close across tiers, so more faces beat bigger faces (Sweat Equity, DTC plan, Nov 2026, not launched yet).

## Whose account

Creators post on an account they own in the niche, so every video gets reach. In our 11-09 test, the plans that paid for videos on brand channels with no audience lost. A new niche account gets a warm-up first: about 30 niche videos watched and engaged with, then two niche posts without the product, before the first paid one. When the user wants ad files only, it's production: flat per video, with the ad use priced.

## Pay

- **Default:** a base per video, plus a bonus counted over each video's first 7 days at multiples of that creator's own usual, with a cap. Our proposal, by `scripts/price.py offer`: +1× the base at 3× their usual, +2× more at 10×, so the bonus caps at 3× the base and a video at 4× (base and bonus). Sizing to their usual keeps the bonus reachable for a creator who usually gets 3K views; @maubaron's per-100K bands pay them close to nothing. The month's money is the bases plus one bonus pool, not every video at its cap: bonuses are paid as videos cross a threshold until the pool is spent, and every offer and draft to a creator says so.
- **Price each creator off their floor:** the median of the 10 worst of their last 30 posts, and what the fee costs per 1,000 views at that level (@maubaron's method; `scripts/price.py floor`).
- **Sold online, with a code or link per creator:** add commission. Commission alone gets turned down.
- Pay weekly for posted videos. Every offer reads per video; a monthly deal is shown as its per-video price.

## The first 30 days

| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | Pick the 3 angles with `/cracked-marketer:ideas`, the creators with `/cracked-marketer:find-creators`; agree the terms below; briefs from `/cracked-marketer:brief` |
| 1-2 | The 12 test videos post, one a day per account at most; each creator's tracked link live |
| 3 | Read each video at 7 days against its creator's usual (below) |
| 4 | Each week from now: 80% of videos repeat what's proven, 20% test something new (@maubaron). Winners go to every creator. Keep or stop each creator at day 14 on reliability and draft quality |

Day 30: the money check. Add creators only where a format won.

## Reading results

- **By where it broke** (@maubaron, for apps): under 1,000 views the opening failed; 1,000-10,000, people left before the product; 100,000 or more with few downloads or sales, the product moment or the ask failed. He gives no read for 10,000-100,000, so judge those against the creator's own usual.
- **Sales or signups per 1,000 views** from each creator's tracked link, from the user's own analytics. Views decide little before about 25 posts on an account. Wispr cancelled a program that got views and few customers.
- **A winner:** 5× its account's usual within 48 hours, or comments asking what or where the product is. It's proven when it holds on two other creators within 7 days. A proven post is an ad candidate: run it through the access code agreed on day one. `/cracked-marketer:content-log` reads this every week.

## Openings (our read of 134 app demos)

- The product is on screen within 3 seconds and the first cut lands by about 3 seconds.
- The hook is said out loud, under 12 words, with a number, a surprise or a problem, and the same words sit on screen from the first frame.
- For an app, film the phone with a second phone: 114 of the 134 did.
- Show the payoff (a face reacting, a result, the finished thing).

## What to agree before anyone films

The fee per video, the bonus thresholds and cap, the pay day; deliverables, due dates and one round of notes; the account and the posting days; the paid-partnership label on every post; ad use (term, fee, the Spark or partnership code); whether they're tied to a rival brand; raw files; no AI version of their face or voice without their yes. List these for the user to agree; their own lawyer or template writes the contract.

## The answer, in order

1. **The plan on one screen:** creators, videos, money per video and in total for the month, what a win looks like by day 30.
2. **The test** (or the winner to spread): the grid, who films what, the weeks.
3. **Pay:** the model and why, each creator's offer per video, then the published deals beside it, each with whose and when. Mark every number as theirs or our proposal.
4. **Whose accounts and the warm-up.**
5. **Reading results and spreading a winner.**
6. **What to agree.**
7. **Next:** the skill that runs each step.

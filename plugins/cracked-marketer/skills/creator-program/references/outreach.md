# How many to message

To end up with N creators filming, the founder has to know how many to message in week 0. Every rate below is someone else's, with whose, when and how sure. The user's own first wave replaces them.

## Plan on about 30 messages for each creator you want filming

That's our planning number, and it's rough: 1 in 10 reply (Cal AI's rate below), and we guess 1 in 3 of those say yes to an offer that already names the pay. Wispr Flow's playbook lands in the same range. At Cal AI's mass rate (about 1 in 100 signed) it would be closer to 100 each; a personal message that names the pay should do better, and the first wave measures it. For 4 filming, that's about 120 messages, and about 120 names: find-creators returns roughly 7 to 25 who fit per turn of up to 60 credits, so price the names with it.

## How to send them

- **In waves.** Send the first 25 to 40. Follow up on day 4 and day 10. On day 11, count the replies and the yeses. The next wave is the creators still needed ÷ (yeses ÷ messages sent). Keep our 1 in 30 until a wave of 25 or more has 2 yeses or more. Save their rate in the program, so the next wave starts from it instead of ours.
- **Email first,** when there's one. Most of Cal AI's outreach was email, because DMs are capped per account. On TikTok, send a few DMs a day.
- **Two follow-ups,** in the same thread: day 4 adds one thing (the due date, or how it's paid), and day 10 closes the loop politely.
- **One more creator than you need,** while their own rate is unknown. Drop anyone who hasn't delivered by week 2 (program.md).

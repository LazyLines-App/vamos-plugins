## Final answer

## Grüns (@grunsdaily) vs. Leafly

**Summary:** Grüns targets greens powder directly. Its pitch is that powders are a chore: scooping, mixing, washing up, one more routine to remember. Paid creators feed its Meta ads. The way to answer is to show Leafly fitting into a busy woman's real day. A general "better health" message doesn't answer them.

They are attacking your category openly. This [anti-powder static ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1618518729859383) opens with "F*CK YOUR GREENS POWDER." It launched Oct 15 and has been running 21 days.

### 1. Organic posts that beat their usual
Their usual: about 27.8K views on TikTok, 10.3K on Instagram and 170 on YouTube Shorts.

**TikTok** (the clearest wins on their own audience)
- [Bucket-hat office prank](https://www.tiktok.com/@grunsdaily/video/7691818319803845918): 111K views, 4× usual. It's a visual gag where coworkers' reactions keep escalating.
- [Customer story, "Legacy Dad"](https://www.tiktok.com/@grunsdaily/video/7694769912530685215): 109K views, 3.9× usual, and over 5 minutes long. It's a real customer's story and the team calling him.
- [Formula fight](https://www.tiktok.com/@grunsdaily/video/7689579359379754270): 72K views, 2.6× usual. One person has a single packet, the other a table full of pill bottles. This one is actually for Immün, their sister brand.
- [Product cheer](https://www.tiktok.com/@grunsdaily/video/7691168066322894110): 69K views, 2.5× usual. A chant turns the number of packs into the joke.

**Instagram** (the big posts come from creator collabs, so collaborators' followers inflate the numbers)
- [Bodega episode](https://www.instagram.com/p/DbGvGCojxpD/): 82K views, 7.9× usual, a collab with @willeygoat and @stanleyws.
- [Back-to-school Target restock](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcB94a-FWro/): 71K views, 6.8× usual. That's below the creator's own usual, so it's shared reach, not a breakout.
- Their paid posts did about normal numbers for each creator: [Phillip Ricke](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeH0j8aCWyf/) at 177K (1.3× his usual) and [Beth Anne Brice](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeILAKiTjZ8/) at 69K (0.99×).

**YouTube:** The wins are relative only, and the numbers are tiny: [Bodega ep. 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h531WqtuDKU) got 1.5K views at 9× usual. Not worth investing in. Stay on IG and TikTok.

The longest-running ads are for their sister brands, not the adult greens gummies. The angles that matter most for you:
- **Unpredictable days (Scheana, 92 days):** It opens on a schedule that keeps changing, then shows the product fitting in. That's very close to your buyer.
- **A specific first-order offer:** $66.65 crossed out, $29.99 first order, "$1.07/day," free gifts and a 30-day guarantee. It renews at $49.99 every 4 weeks, almost exactly your price.
- **The first taste on camera:** [Ava Stanley](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=938534919068365) opens a packet and eats a gummy, and the ad says outright "no mixing or scooping."
- **A separate landing page for each buyer type:** e.g. a [perimenopause ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1792962108675712) that goes to its own page.

### 3. Their creator program
**Confirmed paid partners** (via partnership ads or disclosed posts):
- Adult Grüns: Jordan Farris, Ava Stanley, Krista Horton, [Jenna Bush Hager](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2739150599835081) and [Hannah Brown](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1373892184614847)
- Disclosed IG posts: Phillip Ricke, Beth Anne Brice
- Grüns Kids: Scheana Marie, Chad Michael Murray, Hayley Carling

**How it runs:**
- **Seeding:** An [Archive case study](https://archive.com/customer-stories/gruns) reports outreach to about 650 creators a week. That figure is from their early days.
- **No scripts:** Their [Creators Hub](https://gruns.co/pages/hub) says "No scripts or expectations. You do you," and steers creators toward convenience, taste and enjoyment. So there's no shared script to quote word for word.
- **Affiliate pay:** Creators get affiliate links with a 45% audience discount. Their commission rate isn't public.
- **Pipeline:** gift product → creator posts → clear usage rights → edit → run as partnership ads in several versions.

### 4. What Leafly should copy
1. **"My mornings change every day. Here's where Leafly fits."** A creator shows her real workday plus the full prep. Modeled on Scheana's 92-day ad.
2. **"Leafly is $49. Watch me make it before you decide."** An uncut prep and first sip. This answers their anti-powder attack head on instead of hiding the scoop.
3. **Ideal morning vs. actual morning:** a side-by-side skit, modeled on the 2.6× formula fight.
4. **A real customer's reason to stick with it:** open on her own strongest line, modeled on the 3.9× customer story.
5. **Show exactly what $49 buys:** servings, the product and your guarantee in frame, with renewal terms visible if you sell subscriptions.

**Creator program:** Pick creators who already film realistic busy-day content. Keep gifting separate from paid deliverables, and contract usage rights for partnership ads up front. Give them the claims they can and can't make, then let them use their own words. In paid tests, run idea 1 against idea 2 with the same offer and landing page, and cut or scale on cost to acquire a customer, not views.

**Leave out:** the office comedy, mascots and retail stunts, deep welcome-kit discounts your margins can't carry, and any health claims they make (bloating, perimenopause and so on) unless Leafly's formula supports them.

**The gap they leave:** Grüns' whole argument is that powder is a hassle. A brand that shows the prep honestly and makes it look fast and pleasant turns their attack into your proof.

## Final answer

Yes, post it, but change the first frame: open with the starter jar close to the lens, not just your face. Vamos based that on two similar reels.

**What to change**
- **0–3s:** Hold the jar up to the camera while you say the hook. People should see the starter before they see you.
- **3–18s:** Show each mistake and its fix, one short sentence each.
- **18–20s:** End with "Save this for your next starter feed."
- If a fix needs feeding ratios or caveats, 20 seconds is too tight. Either cover one mistake properly or make the reel longer.

**Hook:** your line is fine as long as these mistakes really can kill a starter. If they only slow it down, Vamos suggests **"Sourdough starter won't rise? Check these 3 mistakes."** It hasn't seen your three mistakes, so it couldn't check whether they're accurate. That part is up to you.

**The two reels it compared yours to**
- [@sab_bakes](https://www.instagram.com/p/DePOYWVRcVC/) (506 followers) got **69,556 views, about 26× their usual**, on Oct 8. It opened on hands uncovering a bubbly starter. The comments asked about feeding ratios, flour and fridge storage, so people clearly want this kind of practical help.
- [Another starter demo](https://www.instagram.com/p/DeZSZ47xtGp/) got **231,167 views, about 11× that account's usual**, on Oct 12. It showed flour going into the jar, mixing, and the risen result. It also offered a free guide, which accounts for some of its comments.

Both back the topic and opening on the starter. Neither tells you how many views yours will get.

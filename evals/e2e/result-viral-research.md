**What Vamos would prioritise for your Instagram:**
1. **Film your next lid lift.** Put your face and the loaf in the same shot, and start recording before you know how it turned out. It's the easiest format here to copy at home, and it's the one that took off on Instagram.
2. **Make one full beginner-help post.** Examples are a small starter experiment with daily progress, or a whole loaf with timestamps plus the dough cues to watch for. Your own bakes are the proof.
3. **Try a discard recipe that skips a hard step**, using the donut post's angle: show the finished bake first, then say what step or equipment it saves you.

All six videos share one thing: **the payoff is something you can see**, and it's promised in the first seconds.
